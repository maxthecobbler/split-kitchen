Split Kitchen

two players · one phone

Sit facing each other. The top half is upside down on purpose — that's the line cook. The bottom half is prep.

Prep taps a bin, taps the board three times to cut it, then taps once more to push it onto the pass counter.

Line cook takes from the counter, drops it on a burner, pulls it off before it burns, adds it to the plate, and serves the matching ticket.

Only three counter slots. Fill them up and prep is stuck.
